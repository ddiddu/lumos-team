1 - establish two prompts (one Summary decision and one with Analytical details).

Maya Torres, Tom Fuller, sharing an operational plan for the SAFENET model:
Weekly SAFE flag updates with an additional 3-5% sample from items the model predicted as not flagged, to reduce selection bias and blind-spot lock-in.
Weekly recall monitoring on newly labeled data. If recall drops below 100% for 2 consecutive weeks, retrain the model or lower the threshold.
If recall stays at 100% for 4+ consecutive weeks, gradually increase the threshold to improve precision.
